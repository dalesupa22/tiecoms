Need help with TieComs? Email admin@tiecoms.com. Our team can help with account access, conversations, shared spaces and files.

Contact support

Tell us what happened, your account email, whether you use iOS, Android or the web, and your app version. You can attach a screenshot after hiding private information. We will never ask for your password or sign-in codes.

Trouble signing in

Use the same method you used to create your account: email and password, Google or Microsoft. If your account belongs to a company, also contact its administrator. If you still cannot sign in, contact us from your account email.

Inappropriate content or behavior

To report a security issue, abuse or inappropriate content, email admin@tiecoms.com. Include the details needed to identify the conversation and explain what happened, without forwarding unnecessary sensitive information.

Privacy and account deletion

Read how to delete your account or email admin@tiecoms.com to exercise your data rights. You can request deletion even if you have uninstalled the app.

Service operator
